Privacy Policy

Ghosted: Her Phone Mystery · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Ghosted: Her Phone Mystery (“the game”, “we”) handles information. In short: the game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or crash-reporting services.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue a case later, the game saves your progress (the current case, the clues you found and your settings such as sound on/off) in the app’s local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing the app’s storage in your device settings.

3. Device permissions

The game may use vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, messages or files. All “phones”, “messages”, “photos”, “locations” and “recordings” shown in the game are fictional content created for the story.

4. Internet access

The game works fully offline and does not send data over the internet.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 16 and over because of its mature themes. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and the game does not collect personal information from anyone.

6. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any. When you download the game from Google Play, Google’s own privacy policy applies to your use of Google Play.

7. Fiction disclaimer

All characters, places, apps, companies, phone numbers and events in the game are fictional. Any resemblance to real persons or services is coincidental.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
